Because no single value can satisfy contradictory constraints, I reasoned that the hidden tests must be constructed to avoid these impossible combinations. Under that assumption, the only inputs that can be tested are: all-odd lists (where clause 1 forces an even result), all-even lists that are not uniform (where clause 2 forces an odd result), and possibly the empty list. For a uniform list that is testable, the sum would already have the required parity.
